# Business Model & Commission Structure

## Overview

This is a **marketplace facilitation model**. We connect buyers with sellers (dealerships and private sellers) and take a flat $100 fee from BOTH parties when a deal closes.

**Key Principle:** Everyone gets paid at the same time. Seller pays us → Buyer pays us → Deal closes. No exceptions.

---

## Commission Breakdown

### Dealership Deals
- **Dealership pays:** $100 (non-refundable, due at time of sale)
- **Buyer pays:** $100 (non-refundable, due at time of sale)
- **Facilitator earnings:** $200 per deal
- **Timing:** Payment due when vehicle title transfers

### Private Seller Deals
- **Private seller pays:** $100 (non-refundable, due at time of sale)
- **Buyer pays:** $100 (non-refundable, due at time of sale)
- **Facilitator earnings:** $200 per deal
- **Timing:** Payment due when vehicle title transfers

---

## Why $100 Per Party?

✅ **Low enough** that buyers don't see it as a "sales fee"  
✅ **Fair to sellers** (dealerships already pay small commissions)  
✅ **Scalable** (1-6 deals/day = $200-$1,200/day potential)  
✅ **Simple** (easy to explain, no negotiations)  
✅ **Professional** (standard in auto facilitation)

---

## Payment Terms

### STRICT RULES

1. **$100 fee is NON-REFUNDABLE** — Once the buyer/seller agrees to work with us, the $100 is committed
2. **Payment due at time of sale** — When title transfers and money changes hands
3. **No partial refunds** — Deal falls through? No refund. Buyer backs out? No refund.
4. **All parties pay simultaneously** — If one doesn't pay, deal doesn't close
5. **Facilitator doesn't process payments** — Parties pay directly to facilitator OR through escrow (TBD)

## Risk Mitigation

- ✅ Terms & Conditions protect all parties
- ✅ Escrow service (optional) for payment security
- ✅ ID verification for buyers/sellers
- ✅ VIN verification to prevent fraud
- ✅ No liability for vehicle conditions (buyer's responsibility)
