# Commission Model - Flexible Representation\n\n## Overview\n\nYour commission depends on **who you represent** in each deal.\n\n---\n\n## Fee Structure\n\n### Scenario 1: Buyer-Side Only (You represent the BUYER)\n- **Buyer pays:** $100 (non-refundable)\n- **Seller pays:** $0\n- **You earn:** $100 per deal\n- **Use case:** Buyer finds vehicle on their own, you help them negotiate/facilitate\n\n### Scenario 2: Seller-Side Only (You represent the SELLER)\n- **Seller pays:** $100 (non-refundable)\n- **Buyer pays:** $0\n- **You earn:** $100 per deal\n- **Use case:** Dealership/private seller brings vehicle to you, you find buyers\n\n### Scenario 3: Both Sides (You represent BOTH)\n- **Buyer pays:** $100 (non-refundable)\n- **Seller pays:** $100 (non-refundable)\n- **You earn:** $200 per deal\n- **Use case:** You source vehicle from seller, match with buyer, facilitate entire deal\n\n---\n\n## How to Track This\n\n### In Your Inquiry Form, Ask:\n\n**\"How did you find this opportunity?\"**\n\n1. ☐ Buyer contacted me about a vehicle (Buyer-side only) → $100 from buyer\n2. ☐ Seller asked me to find a buyer (Seller-side only) → $100 from seller  \n3. ☐ I found both the vehicle AND the buyer (Both sides) → $100 from each\n\n### In Your Database/Google Sheet, Add Column:\n\n**\"Representation Type\"**\n- `buyer_only` = $100 from buyer\n- `seller_only` = $100 from seller\n- `both_sides` = $100 + $100\n\n---\n\n## Revenue Examples\n\n### Conservative Month (2 deals/day, 6 days/week)\n\n| Deal Type | Per Deal | Deals/Week | Weekly | Monthly |\n|-----------|----------|-----------|--------|----------|\n| Buyer-only | $100 | 2 | $200 | $800 |\n| Seller-only | $100 | 3 | $300 | $1,200 |\n| Both-sides | $200 | 1 | $200 | $800 |\n| **TOTAL** | -
